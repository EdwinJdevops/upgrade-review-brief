## Entry requirements

- [ ] Publish one DEV submission using the linked official template before the deadline. The first entry is the one considered if more are submitted.
- [ ] Include tags `devchallenge` and `hf26challenge`.
- [ ] Use a new project and repository started and completed within this challenge's entry period. Existing-project pull requests do not qualify.
- [ ] Put open-source AI at the core: open weights, an open-source AI harness/framework, local inference, or a combination.
- [ ] Fit Slow and Steady by automating a task, reducing effort, helping relaxation or making life easier.
- [ ] Explain why open innovation matters for this implementation.
- [ ] Include a deployed demo or video and a code link or embed. Both a live link and recorded demonstration are our internal target; the rules allow either demo form.
- [ ] Identify Best Use of Render in Prize Categories and meaningfully use Render.
- [ ] Use English for prize judging.
- [ ] Credit non-trivial prior work and third-party components.
- [ ] If a team contributed, publish once and list contributing DEV handles. The FAQ allows teams up to four.
- [ ] Note commits after the official deadline in the README.
- [ ] Confirm entrant eligibility, account standing, rights to the entry and agreement to the general rules. Source code cannot certify personal eligibility or legal representations.
- [ ] Do not present a prize as guaranteed. The specific rules limit a participant to one prize in this contest.

Render category wording: "Use Render as your project's AI runtime". The source also permits an agent front end or named agent runtimes. This entry targets the AI runtime route.

## Template sections

Use the exact linked template, retaining these section titles:

- What I Built
- Demo
- Code
- How I Built It
- Why Does Open Innovation Matter?
- Prize Categories, when entering partner categories

My Agent Session and The Lazy Payoff are optional. Remove them rather than manufacture a session link or effort measurements. The template includes the challenge attribution line and team instructions. Preserve attribution in the draft.

## Judging

Writing quality has the greatest stated weight. Theme relevance, creativity and technical execution are also assessed. Meaningful partner use is assessed for partner categories. No numerical scoring weights are published in the retrieved rules.

AI coding assistance is allowed if other requirements are met. No OpenAI product is mandatory. Do not claim Copilot, Backboard, Tinker or a domain that was not used.
